The GPL-2.0-only integration patch and exact hashes are in rtx/.
The patch adds strict target matching, separate read-only and transaction
builds, and manual boot confirmation. The engine builds locally from the
pinned source. NVIDIA-derived assets are generated locally and their license
and notice are retained in the local build directory.

The starter ZIP contains no upstream EFI executable, GPU firmware, VBIOS/PROM,
Windows driver, CUDA DLL, raw diagnostic capture or machine-specific profile.
Recipients obtain the pinned upstream sources locally. Those sources retain
their own licenses, attribution, notices and change history. Firmware generation
must retain its generated NVIDIA-LICENSE and upstream firmware NOTICE.

No upstream endorsement is claimed. A preparation check is not hardware
compatibility approval. Exact reviewed capture, profile, asset and actual-input host-test gates decide
whether a local USB package can be built. Physical dual-card behavior remains
unverified.
