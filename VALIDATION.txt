- Actual USB staging or a new physical unlock/recovery boot through this helper.

are excluded. The public ZIP contains no EFI, firmware, PROM/VBIOS or driver
binary. Test fixtures and their synthetic results are not hardware performance
measurements. No application-generation comparison tool is included.
